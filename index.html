    Health, illness & injury risks

    Exercise can help many people, but it is not a replacement for medical advice. Stop and get help for chest pain, fainting, severe breathing trouble, or pain after injury.

    Sprains & strains

    Sudden twisting or poor form can hurt muscles or ligaments. Rest, use the safe RICE steps if appropriate, and ask a health professional if pain is strong or lasting.

    Overtraining

    Too much exercise can cause tiredness, poor sleep, soreness, and lower performance. Include rest days and easier sessions.

    Heat illness

    Hot environments can cause dehydration or heat illness. Drink water, rest in shade, and stop for headache, confusion, vomiting, or weakness.

    Head or neck injury

    Flips and gymnastic moves can cause serious injury. Never practice without trained supervision, correct equipment, and landing mats.

    When to get help

    Contact emergency services for severe symptoms. Ask a parent or trusted health professional before exercise if you have a heart, joint, breathing, or long-term condition.

    Life expectancy

    This app cannot responsibly predict lifespan. Genetics, environment, medical history, and luck all matter. It does not estimate illness risk or predict a future diagnosis.
