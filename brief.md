## One-liner

(MVP Plan Worksheet, Section 1)
My app lets people determine the best workout routines, potential illnesses they might get, and optionally give estimates of lifespans, based on information input of their BMI, genetics, race, age, health, routines, physical history, gender and diet, and cross-referencing real-life examples of others, with all of the inputs being optional, however also having a confidence level in the estimates based on how much data they were able to accurately calculate and their confidence in the result. The app should also be able to play ambient sounds and have fun minigames and advisor inside to use during workout routines and also teach you how to properly carry out said workouts based on real information and examples. The app also should have a progress bar rank level to measure how well you are doing and a task manager to keep track of your goals and manage time spent on them.

## Musts + done when

(MVP Plan Worksheet, Section 3. Build them one at a time.)

- Must 1: be able to accurately collate all the info and data provided and give a good response, estimate or plan
  - Done when: I put in my BMI and other information and I see good routines and estimates.
- Must 2: be able to take examples or direct people to other relevant places on the internet to what they are asking
  - Done when: I ask for how to perform an exercise and I see explanations on how to do it and links to videos from the internet.
- Must 3: be able to facilitate the activities too
  - Done when: I start a workout and I see a progress meter and hear ambient music and sounds during it.
